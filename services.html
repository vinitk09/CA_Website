Valuation Services
Valuation has become an integral component of the modern business environment. Numerous business transactions require valuation in accordance with applicable statutes and regulatory frameworks.

Regulatory Framework:
Section 247 of the Companies Act, 2013 mandates that all valuations required under the Act must be conducted by Registered Valuers. Similarly, under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, 2016 (IBC), only Registered Valuers are eligible to undertake valuation assignments. The Insolvency and Bankruptcy Board of India (IBBI) is the authority empowered to grant registration to valuers.

SPARK is committed to delivering high-quality valuation services in compliance with applicable laws and professional standards.

Our Valuation Services:
• Valuation under the Companies Act, 2013
• Valuation under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code
• Valuation for mergers and acquisitions
• Valuation for financial reporting
• Business valuation for strategic planning
• Valuation of intangible assets
