GST15 May 2026

Important Updates on GST Returns & Compliance

Key filing checkpoints, reconciliation priorities and practical controls for businesses managing periodic GST compliance.

Businesses should review outward supply reporting, input tax credit matching, reverse charge items and e-way bill records before finalizing periodic returns.

GST compliance works best when return filing is treated as a monthly control process rather than a last-day activity. The finance team should reconcile books, e-invoices, e-way bills, GSTR-1 and GSTR-2B before filing.

Important areas include invoice classification, HSN reporting, credit eligibility, vendor follow-up, reverse charge liability, blocked credits and amendments from earlier periods. A documented checklist helps reduce avoidable notices and mismatches.

For larger businesses, GST review should also include branch-wise registration status, place of supply checks, export documentation, LUT renewal, credit notes, debit notes, advances, exempt supplies, blocked credits, common credit reversals, job-work movement and vendor compliance history. The review should be supported by a monthly file that connects books of account, e-invoice records, e-way bill data, GSTR-1, GSTR-3B, GSTR-2B, payment challans and management approvals. This helps the finance team identify differences early, prepare explanations for unusual variances, and maintain a clear evidence trail for notices, departmental queries, audit observations and internal reporting.

  • Reconcile sales register with e-invoice and GSTR-1 data.
  • Match input tax credit with GSTR-2B and vendor filings.
  • Review reverse charge, exempt turnover and credit reversals.
  • Keep working papers for amendments, credit notes and adjustments.
Taxation10 May 2026

CBDT Issues Guidelines on Income Tax Returns

A concise view of documentation, due dates and review areas to keep tax return preparation more predictable.

Tax teams should align return preparation with Form 26AS, AIS/TIS, books of account and supporting schedules before filing.

Income tax return preparation requires careful review of accounting records, tax deduction statements, financial statements and statutory disclosures. Early reconciliation reduces the risk of defective returns and post-filing queries.

Businesses should verify depreciation, disallowances, TDS/TCS credits, related-party transactions, brought-forward losses and reporting under applicable audit clauses. Proper documentation also supports future assessments and internal review.

  • Compare Form 26AS, AIS and TIS with books of account.
  • Validate deductions, exemptions and tax audit adjustments.
  • Review capital gains, foreign assets and high-value transactions where applicable.
  • Maintain computation notes and supporting evidence.
Corporate Law09 May 2026

MCA Notifies Changes in Companies Rules

Recent compliance themes for companies, directors and finance teams tracking MCA reporting obligations.

Companies should periodically review board records, statutory registers, beneficial ownership information and event-based filings.

Corporate law compliance is a continuous responsibility. Changes in company particulars, director details, capital structure, deposits, loans, related-party transactions and significant beneficial ownership may trigger filing requirements.

A structured compliance calendar helps management avoid late fees and governance gaps. Board minutes, registers, disclosures and resolutions should be maintained in a manner that supports both statutory filings and stakeholder review.

  • Track annual and event-based MCA filings.
  • Update statutory registers and director disclosures.
  • Review related-party transactions and approval thresholds.
  • Maintain board and shareholder records with supporting documents.
MCA Updates28 Apr 2026

Annual Filing Readiness for Private Companies

Board records, registers and filing data points that should be reviewed before annual compliance work begins.

Early readiness reduces pressure during annual filing season and improves the quality of financial and governance disclosures.

Annual filing readiness starts before the financial statements are finalized. Companies should review director KYC, shareholding, registers, financial statement attachments, auditor details and board approvals.

Private companies should also check whether any events during the year require additional filings. A pre-filing review helps identify missing resolutions, old registers, unrecorded transfers and gaps in disclosure.

  • Review AOC-4 and MGT-7 data points in advance.
  • Confirm board meeting and AGM documentation.
  • Check director, auditor and shareholding details.
  • Prepare attachments in prescribed formats.
Audit21 Apr 2026

Internal Controls That Strengthen Audit Outcomes

How clear control ownership, evidence trails and periodic reviews improve audit readiness through the year.

Strong controls make audits faster, cleaner and more useful for management decision-making.

Audit quality improves when controls are designed, owned and evidenced throughout the year. Businesses should identify critical processes, define approvals, retain documentation and review exceptions periodically.

Controls around revenue, purchases, inventory, payroll, statutory dues and bank reconciliations deserve special attention. Management review controls should be documented with clear evidence of review and resolution.

  • Assign control owners for key business processes.
  • Retain evidence for approvals, reconciliations and reviews.
  • Track exceptions and corrective action timelines.
  • Use periodic internal reviews to stay audit-ready.
Advisory12 Apr 2026

Finance Dashboards for Founder-Led Businesses

Metrics and reporting routines that help owners understand cash flow, profitability and operational risk.

A concise dashboard can help leadership act earlier on margin movement, receivables pressure and cash runway.

Founder-led businesses need dashboards that are simple enough to review regularly and deep enough to support decisions. The best dashboards connect financial performance with operational drivers.

Key metrics may include cash balance, receivable ageing, payables, gross margin, operating expenses, statutory dues, order pipeline and working capital cycle. The goal is not more data, but clearer decisions.

  • Track cash runway, collections and payment priorities.
  • Review margins by product, service line or location.
  • Use ageing reports to manage receivable risk.
  • Compare monthly performance against budget and forecast.
RERA04 Apr 2026

RERA Compliance Checks for Developers

Practical filing, escrow and reporting considerations for real estate projects and promoter teams.

Developers should connect project accounting, customer collections and statutory reporting in one review cycle.

RERA compliance requires accurate project-level tracking. Promoters should ensure that collections, withdrawals, project costs, completion status and customer communications are supported by proper records.

Regular compliance review helps avoid mismatches between project accounts and regulatory disclosures. Escrow utilization, certificates and periodic updates should be reviewed before submission.

  • Maintain project-wise books and cost records.
  • Review escrow withdrawals and certificate support.
  • Track customer advances, agreements and allotments.
  • Align website and portal disclosures with actual status.
International Tax28 Mar 2026

Cross-Border Tax Points for Indian Businesses

Areas to review when overseas transactions, withholding, transfer pricing and documentation overlap.

International transactions need coordinated review of contracts, tax withholding, pricing and reporting obligations.

Cross-border transactions can create income tax, withholding, GST, FEMA and transfer pricing implications. Businesses should review the nature of services, place of supply, treaty position, documentation and payment terms.

Early review helps avoid gross-up disputes, delayed remittances and audit questions. Related-party transactions should be supported by agreements, benchmarking and contemporaneous documentation where applicable.

  • Review withholding tax and treaty eligibility.
  • Assess transfer pricing documentation requirements.
  • Check FEMA and remittance documentation.
  • Align accounting entries with contract terms.
Judicial Updates20 Mar 2026

Recent Tax Rulings and Business Impact

Selected judicial developments that may influence assessment strategy, documentation and risk review.

Judicial updates should be translated into practical action points for documentation and future positions.

Tax rulings often clarify interpretation, evidence expectations and procedural safeguards. Businesses should evaluate whether recent decisions affect existing positions, assessments, appeals or future transactions.

A ruling may not apply mechanically to every case, but it can guide risk assessment and documentation strategy. Management should consult advisors before changing positions based on judicial developments.

  • Identify rulings relevant to current tax positions.
  • Review evidence gaps in ongoing assessments or appeals.
  • Update internal tax notes where interpretation changes.
  • Document management rationale for positions taken.
Budget12 Mar 2026

Budget Highlights for Finance Teams

Key policy signals and action points for businesses reviewing budgets, forecasts and compliance calendars.

Finance teams should convert budget announcements into a structured review of tax, cash flow and compliance impact.

Budget announcements can affect tax rates, deduction rules, compliance timelines, incentives and sector-specific policy. Businesses should identify applicable changes and evaluate their financial and operational impact.

A practical review should cover cash flow, pricing, contracts, payroll, investments, statutory calendars and system changes. Early planning helps teams avoid last-minute implementation pressure.

  • Map announcements to business-specific action items.
  • Update tax computations, budgets and forecasts.
  • Review contract, payroll and invoicing impact.
  • Track effective dates and transition provisions.
